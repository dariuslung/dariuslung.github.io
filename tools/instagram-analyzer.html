How to use the analyzer?
  1. Go to Instagram Settings > Accounts Center > Your Information and permissions.
  2. Select Export your information > Create export.
  3. Choose Some of your information > scroll down and select Followers and following.
  4. Select Export to device, set Format to JSON (mandatory), and request export.
  5. Once ready, download the ZIP archive from Instagram and upload it here (or unzip and upload followers_1.json & following.json).
