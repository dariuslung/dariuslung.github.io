Tools

A collection of custom utilities and mini-apps.

Instagram Follower Analyzer

Find accounts that don't follow you back, accounts you don't follow back, and mutual followers.

Spotify Playlist Sorter

Sort your Spotify playlists by their true original release dates using open metadata from MusicBrainz.

Weighted RNG

A tool to generate random outcomes based on weighted probabilities.
